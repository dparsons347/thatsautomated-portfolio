## What we build
Automations between the tools a small business already uses: web forms, email, CRMs (HubSpot, GoHighLevel), accounting (QuickBooks), scheduling and field service apps, Google Workspace, Slack, and anything with an API. Built in n8n, Make or Zapier, with custom Python where a no-code step is not enough. Every build includes error alerts. Pricing depends on scope and is quoted after a short call.

## Support hours
Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm Central. Email sent after hours is read the next business morning. Clients on a care plan who have a workflow down can put "URGENT" in the subject line and it is looked at within 2 business hours.

## A workflow stopped because a login expired
Google, Microsoft and HubSpot connections sometimes expire when a password changes or an admin revokes access. The workflow pauses and an alert posts in the shared Slack channel. To fix it: open the link in the alert, click "Reconnect", sign in with the same account that was used originally, then reply to this email so we can re-run anything that was missed. Nothing is lost while it is paused; missed items are queued.

## Where error alerts go
Every workflow we build posts failures to the #automation-alerts channel in the client's Slack (or by email if the client does not use Slack). Each alert names the workflow, the step that failed, and a link to the run.
